# Account and onboarding flow

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing Clayface workbench identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A new user makes one decision at a time and reaches their own first design. The user explicitly requested sequential name, project, and design steps before the editor.

OWN-WORLD: Warm neutral surfaces, Geist, clay primary actions, modest radii, visible focus. Readable 14–16px form fields extend the existing compact workbench into a focused setup surface.

STORY: Create an account, enter a name, describe the project and pages, choose a direction, then enter a private saved editor. Returning users resume their saved step or open their project. Security settings sit outside setup.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet brand header; a narrow form with one heading and primary action. Onboarding adds a three-step progress rail on desktop and a compact progress row on mobile. Errors stay next to the action with entered values preserved.

FORM: Explicit user-selected sequential setup, within the established design system. No concept seed: workflow and identity were specified.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
